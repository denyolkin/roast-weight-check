Roast Weight Check

Inspect Artisan .alog weights on this device. Choose up to 20 files, each no larger than 1 MiB. Only gram (g) weights are supported: positive measurements must be between 0.000001 and 1,000,000,000 g. Zero and missing measurements stay unknown. The files are read locally and are never uploaded.

Selecting files replaces the current report. The demo is authored test data, not a customer roast. Clearing removes the selected files and report from this page.
